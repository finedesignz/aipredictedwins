# Market Participant Personas

## Retail Trader
- Information: Twitter, Reddit, mainstream news, friends
- Risk tolerance: Variable, often overestimates
- Biases: Strong recency bias, herding, FOMO
- Trading behavior: Momentum-chasing, panic selling, position sizing errors
- Typical conviction: High on trending topics, low on complex events

## Institutional Investor
- Information: Bloomberg, proprietary research, expert networks
- Risk tolerance: Moderate, governed by mandates
- Biases: Anchoring to models, overconfidence in process
- Trading behavior: Systematic, size-aware, contrarian at extremes
- Typical conviction: Moderate, hedged

## Financial Media Commentator
- Information: Broad but shallow, narrative-driven
- Risk tolerance: N/A (influences others, doesn't trade directly)
- Biases: Availability heuristic, dramatic framing, false balance
- Impact: Amplifies momentum, creates availability cascades
- Typical conviction: Strong on camera, private uncertainty

## Policy Expert / Academic
- Information: Deep domain knowledge, research, insider contacts
- Risk tolerance: Conservative
- Biases: Overconfidence in own models, underweight of political dynamics
- Trading behavior: Slow to act, high conviction when they do
- Typical conviction: Very high in their domain, humble outside it

## Political Operative / Insider
- Information: Non-public signals, party dynamics, vote counts
- Risk tolerance: Information-dependent
- Biases: Optimism about their side, discounting opposition
- Trading behavior: Early mover on private information
- Typical conviction: High when they have information, volatile otherwise

## Quantitative Trader / Algorithm
- Information: Historical data, statistical models, real-time feeds
- Risk tolerance: Model-defined
- Biases: Overfitting to historical patterns, fragile to regime changes
- Trading behavior: Fast, systematic, size-disciplined
- Typical conviction: Based on signal strength, not narrative
