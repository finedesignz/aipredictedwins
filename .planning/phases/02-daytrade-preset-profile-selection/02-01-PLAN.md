CRITICAL — orchestrator module-load coupling (src/alpaca_orchestrator.py ~line 52-76):
The module sets `PROFILE = SWING` at import, and the env-default constants below it
(`MAX_POSITION_PCT`, `MIN_CONFLUENCE`, `MIN_SHORT_CONFLUENCE`, `CYCLE_SLEEP_SECONDS`)
read `PROFILE.*` AT IMPORT TIME via `os.environ.get(KEY, str(PROFILE.field))`.
Therefore BOT_PROFILE MUST be resolved on the line that currently reads
`PROFILE = SWING`, before those constants are computed. Do NOT move resolution into
main() — that would be too late and break the env-default chain.
