<objective>
Add a deterministic pre-trade fee/slippage gate (FEE-01). Before sizing an approved
candidate, skip it when the expected move to its soft take-profit target does not clear
round-trip cost `2 * taker_fee + slippage_buffer`. Prevents thin intraday trades that
cannot overcome fees.

Purpose: stop fee-churn on the 5-min daytrade cadence without touching swing behavior.
Output: `src/fee_gate.py` helper, gate inserted into 4 entry blocks (orchestrator long/short,
bot_thread long/short), tests.
</objective>
