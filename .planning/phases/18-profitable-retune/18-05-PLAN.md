<objective>
Give the backtester the three knobs the sweep varies, and close the two fidelity gaps the sweep
depends on.

Purpose: **TUNE-03's entire claim is "validated against the existing backtest."** But the engine's
entry predicate is one conjunct of the live bot's eight. Two of the missing seven are load-bearing
for *this* phase:

- **`entry_allowed`** — the quarantine gate. Without it, the sweep's quarantine arm tests nothing.
- **`rsi_ceiling`** — the live bot rejects overbought longs; the engine does not. Without it the
  backtest takes trades the bot refuses, inflating trade counts *at exactly the confluence levels
  being swept*.

The other four gaps (loss cooldown, 4H trend filter, shorts, the soft/trailing/advisor exit stack)
are **declared, not closed** — closing them is a new strategy model, not a retune. `18-BACKTEST.md`
must carry them so the holdout number is read as *"the long-only, hard-exit lower bound"* and not as
a promise about live P&L.

Output: three new `PhaseConfig` fields, two engine guards, four CLI args, one hard `parser.error`.
Turns VALIDATION cases 14-17, 19-22 GREEN.
