<objective>
Ship the read-only report CLI and generate the Phase-18 evidence.

Purpose: turn the trustworthy dataset into defensible per-symbol / per-bot numbers — winners and losers as
concrete figures, with the sample guard visible and **every data defect stated out loud rather than
absorbed**:

- `zero_pnl_total` — position-closed rows with `pnl == 0.0`, the external-exit sentinel
  (`src/alpaca_orchestrator.py:167-176`). Not losses. Not trades.
- `gross_pnl_rows_total` — counted rows whose `fees` is NULL, i.e. whose `pnl` is probably **GROSS**
  (`src/bot_c/strategy.py:393-395`, `src/trend_strategy.py:172-173`). Their `realized_pnl` is **not
  fee-adjusted**, and `total_fees` under-reports drag for exactly those bots.
- `sign_suspect_rows` (**R3-W2**) — of those, the ones with `side <> 'buy'`. The gross writers compute
  `(current_price - entry) * q` with **no side handling** (unlike `src/pnl.py:10`), so on a short the
  **sign is inverted** — and the row is still counted as a win or a loss. **A losing short reads as a
  winner.** Worse than "gross".
- `null_pnl_total` — resolution defects.
- **The COUNT/RATE divergence** (**R3-B2**) — not a P&L delta. See below.
- The Rev-2 **W2** finding — `src/db.py:201` uses a fourth status-set spelling in the live entry cooldown.

**R3-B2 — why there is no `realized_pnl` delta in this report.** `src/db.py:252-259` `get_realized_pnl` sums
`(r["pnl"] or 0.0)` over the **same** status set. A NULL contributes `0.0`; a sentinel zero contributes
`0.0`. `symbol_stats` **excludes** both — removing exactly `0.0 + 0.0`. **The delta is therefore identically
`0.00` and can never fire**; printing it would tell an operator the data is clean, which is the exact lie
this milestone exists to kill. The damage is in the **denominator**: `src/db.py:228-229`
`losses = resolved - wins` books **every sentinel zero and every NULL as a LOSS**, and `avg_pnl` divides by
`resolved`. **That** is the number on the dashboard. So the report prints the count/rate divergence.

The report **ANNOTATES**; it does not **DECIDE**. Phase 18 (TUNE-01/TUNE-03) consumes this and owns the
`quarantined_symbols` write.

Output: scripts/symbol_report.py + EVIDENCE.md (turns VALIDATION cases 22-23 GREEN; the case-20 fence — which
also asserts the argparse surface carries no mutating flag — must stay green).
