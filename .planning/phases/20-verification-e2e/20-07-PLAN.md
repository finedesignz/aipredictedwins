Purpose: this is the final phase of the milestone, and its job is to say whether any of phases 11-19
actually reconciles against real money. **That answer must be a measurement.** Two structural traps
would let it be green and empty instead. This plan is built around closing both.

1. **The window may not have opened yet.** `T0` is written only by the manager's hourly reconcile tick,
   in prod, running Phase-20 code — after migration 020 is applied and the code is deployed. Before
   that, the `reconciliation_anchor` table does not exist and every bot reports `NO_ANCHOR /
   table_absent`. And even once it does exist, `MIN_WINDOW_SAMPLE = 20` resolved trades per bot puts a
   real verdict **days to weeks** out. **So the plan branches explicitly (Task 0) and never writes
   "Achieved: within tolerance on the post-`T0` window" on a day when that is false.**

2. **The credentials may be missing.** The repo's `.env` has a bare `ALPACA_API_KEY`, no `_A`/`_B`/`_C`
   suffixes and no `DATABASE_URL` — while `_client_for_bot` requires the per-bot form and raises without
   it. Without a fetch, both measurements degrade to `NOT_RUN`, and **a milestone about eliminating
   unverified numbers would close having produced none. `NOT_RUN` closes nothing.** Credentials are a
   blocking pre-flight gate.

And then it stops. The backfill is fixed, tested, documented — and **left unarmed** behind a blocking
human checkpoint.
