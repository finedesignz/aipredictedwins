<objective>
Fix `src/backfill.py`'s two defects and **leave it unarmed**.

Purpose: `src/backfill.py` is the tool that would one day repair the 395 historical `pnl = 0.0`
sentinel rows. Run with `--apply` **today** it would instead **corrupt every genuinely-held open
position** — resolving live positions as `closed` with fabricated P&L, or as `unresolvable`
(`pnl = NULL`), which drops them out of `get_open_alpaca_positions` (src/db.py:160) and thereby
**kills their stop-loss, take-profit and exit advisor while the position is still live at Alpaca**.

Two defects, both in the same function:
1. **The slash mismatch.** `:148` builds `live_symbols` from Alpaca's raw slashless `pos.symbol`;
   `:72` and `:155` compare a **slashed** `row["symbol"]` against it. `"BTC/USD" in {"BTCUSD"}` is
   False for **every** held position, always. The `unchanged` arm is unreachable in production.
2. **The `None` third door.** `:148`'s `or []` coerces a **failed** `get_positions()` call to an
   empty set. An Alpaca outage therefore makes the entire book look vanished.

Output: `src/backfill.py` with both compare sites normalized via `src.universe.normalize` and the
`None` sentinel preserved. **No `--apply` run. No write to prod. No new entrypoint.**
</objective>
