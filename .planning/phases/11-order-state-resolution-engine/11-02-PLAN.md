<objective>
Add the order-resolution engine to BotThread and prove it with the pytest suite. Persist
`order_id`/`order_type`/`status='submitted'` at every submission, record submit exceptions as
`rejected`, then each cycle (and once at startup) poll pending orders from the DB and transition them
to a terminal state: `open` on fill (incl. partial), terminal non-position
(`canceled`/`expired`/`rejected`, pnl=0) otherwise, and cancel-then-recheck resting limit orders past
the timeout.

Purpose: Closes PNL-01 (no silent drops — every order reaches a recorded terminal state) and PNL-04
(forward resolution rate ≈100%). PositionMonitor boundary stays intact — it still owns position exits.
Output: `tests/test_order_resolution.py` (Wave 0, written first) + resolver in `src/bot_thread.py`.
</objective>
