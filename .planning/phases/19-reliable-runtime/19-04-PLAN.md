<objective>
Make the numbers honest. A `pnl = 0.0` sentinel stops being counted as a loss, at every site that
reads one — and the paper gate stops being evaluated against a hardcoded $100k.

Purpose: 395 historical rows carry `pnl = 0.0`. `0.0` is NOT NULL, so Phase 18's `AND pnl IS NOT NULL`
filter passes them straight through, and every reader then scores `(r["pnl"] or 0) > 0 -> False` and
books them as **losses** — roughly 60% of the closed-row population is a fabricated loss in the
headline. The gate that guards live trading is being evaluated against that number, on top of a
hardcoded bankroll.

Output: one predicate, five call sites, an `unresolved` count reported beside wins/losses, and the
`100_000.0 * len(bot_ids)` literal gone. **Zero rows in `alpaca_trades` are modified.**
</objective>
